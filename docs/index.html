Methodology & Metric Definitions

Scoring & Conviction

Tool Score (0–100)
Weighted composite: Fundamentals (PE, ROE, D/E, revenue/earnings growth) 40% + Technicals (RSI, MA crossover, volume surge) 30% + Momentum (3/6 month returns, trend) 20% + Sector tailwind 10%
Adjusted Score
Tool Score minus Devil's Advocate penalties. Penalties: Valuation (PEG > 2 = -10 to -15), Debt Trap (D/E > 100 = -12 to -20), Promoter Flags (<25% holding = -15), Sector Headwind (0 to -10), Pump/Dump (micro-cap volatility = -5 to -20)
Conviction Levels
HIGH ≥ 75 | MODERATE 60–74 | LOW 45–59 | REJECTED < 45

Critic Agent (Independent)

Critic Score (0–100)
7-factor independent assessment using its own yfinance data: Precedent Analysis, Cash Flow Quality, Insider Activity Patterns, Valuation Reasonableness, Liquidity Risk, Macro Alignment, Business Survival Probability
Critic Verdict
AGREE = critic score ≥ 60 & aligns with tool | PARTIAL = some merit but reservations | DISAGREE = fundamentally different conclusion

Fundamental Metrics

PE Ratio
Price / Earnings per Share — lower is cheaper, but growth stocks may justify higher PE
PB Ratio
Price / Book Value — below 1 may indicate undervaluation
ROE
Return on Equity — measures profitability relative to shareholder equity. >15% is strong
D/E Ratio
Debt / Equity — measures leverage. >100 is high risk, <50 is comfortable
Promoter Holding
% of shares held by promoters/founders. >50% = high skin in game, <25% = red flag

Technical Metrics

RSI (14-day)
Relative Strength Index — <30 oversold (buy signal), >70 overbought (caution)
MA Crossover
Price crossing above 50-day Moving Average — bullish breakout signal
Volume Surge
% increase in 20-day avg volume vs 50-day avg. >100% = institutional interest
Breakout (BO badge)
Independent scanner detected: volume breakout, MA crossover, RSI recovery from oversold, or near 52-week high
